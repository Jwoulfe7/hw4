Check the First

The Bulldog checks the stock room

On the Basic Hoodie Big Yale product page, the shopper asked: How many mediums of this one are left, and what does it cost? Any in XL?

What this proves The agent looked up live stock and price with its database tools, worked out which item "this one" meant from the page the shopper was on, and reported the exact numbers. The Fact-Checker then confirmed the quoted price matched the catalogue before the reply was shown.
The database says basic-hoodie-big-yale: price 68.0; stock XS=15, S=5, M=5, L=8, XL=2, XXL=25. That matches the reply exactly.

Honest about its inventory the way old families are honest about their boats: it states the facts, gives the exact figure, and never pretends there's one more in the boathouse than there is.

Check the Second

Ask about hoodies, and the cards appear

From the Home page, the shopper asked the chat: What hoodies do you have?

What this proves The agent searched the catalogue and returned structured product matches (the API contract). The front end rendered them as product cards on the page itself, not just in the chat. Clicking a card (the Fencing Left Chest Hoodie) still opened the single-item page from Problem 3.
The database says All six cards show $68.00, and their size chips match in-stock inventory exactly. For example, Champion Reverse Weave Hoodie 1 is S, M, L, XXL (XS and XL are sold out), and Fencing Left Chest Hoodie is S, M, XL.

Six hoodies, laid out like sweaters on the window seat at Mory's: properly folded, correctly priced, and not one of them in a color you'd have to explain at Thanksgiving.

Check the Third

The Valet, summoned with a keystroke

On the Products page, the shopper pressed /, typed saybrook, pressed ↓ once, then Enter. This is a Problem 9 usability feature; see output/usability.md, §1.

What this proves A shopper can get from any page to an exact item in a few keystrokes, with no scrolling and no AI call. Pressing Enter opened /products/saybrook-logo-t-shirt, and the Valet closed itself. The "In your size" tags show the second Problem 9 feature, the Tailor's Ledger, working too.
The database says Saybrook College Crewneck $58.00 (M=15), Saybrook Logo T Shirt $32.00 (M=12), Saybrook Sweater Fleece Jacket $98.00 (M=8). All three are in stock in M, as shown.

The Valet does what good staff always has: it appears the moment it's summoned, remembers your size without being asked twice, and leaves without a word once you've found what you came for.
